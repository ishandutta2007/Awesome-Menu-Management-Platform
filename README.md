# Awesome-Menu-Management-Platform

## Top Menu Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Digital Menus, Online Ordering Menus, Menu Sync Across Channels, QR Menus & Restaurant Menu Publishing*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Menu Management**. These systems help restaurants create, update, and publish digital menus across websites, QR codes, online ordering, delivery aggregators, and in-venue displays—often with centralized control and channel synchronization.



**Examples** include Popmenu, MustHaveMenus, SinglePlatform, Owner.com, Menubly, Deliverect Menu Sync, Otter Menu, MenuDrive, Allset Business, and GloriaFood (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Dedicated menu-management platforms are mostly commercial. Practical open options include **URY** (open restaurant management on ERPNext), simple digital-menu web apps, and self-hosted QR/menu generators. Full multi-channel menu sync with delivery marketplaces remains largely proprietary. This section lists the strongest available open resources.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Popmenu](https://www.popmenu.com/)**  

  Restaurant growth platform with interactive digital menus, online ordering, marketing, and brand-focused menu experiences.



- **[MustHaveMenus](https://www.musthavemenus.com/)**  

  Menu design and publishing tool popular for creating printable and digital restaurant menus from templates.



- **[SinglePlatform](https://www.singleplatform.com/)**  

  Menu and location data platform that helps restaurants publish accurate menus to major online directories and discovery sites.



- **[Owner.com](https://www.owner.com/)**  

  Direct online ordering and restaurant website platform with strong menu management, marketing automation, and commission-free ordering options.



- **[Menubly](https://www.menubly.com/)**  

  Digital menu and QR-code menu solution for restaurants seeking easy menu publishing and updates.



- **[Deliverect Menu Sync](https://www.deliverect.com/)**  

  Menu and order-management capabilities within Deliverect that synchronize menus across delivery channels and POS systems at scale.



- **[Otter Menu](https://www.tryotter.com/)**  

  Menu and order aggregation tools within the Otter platform for multi-location and multi-channel restaurant operations.



- **[MenuDrive](https://www.menudrive.com/)**  

  Online menu and ordering platform that helps restaurants publish menus and accept orders directly.



- **[Allset Business](https://allset.com/)**  

  Restaurant platform supporting digital menus, ordering, and table-related guest experiences.



- **[GloriaFood](https://www.gloriafood.com/)**  

  Free and paid online ordering and menu management solution used by many independent restaurants for websites and QR ordering.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[URY – Open Source Restaurant Management](https://github.com/ury-erp/ury)**  

  FOSS restaurant management system built on ERPNext, including centralized menu management, recipes/BOM, outlet-level pricing and availability, and POS integration.



- **[Digital Menu open web apps](https://github.com/)**  

  Community projects that turn spreadsheets or simple CMS data into responsive digital/QR menus for restaurants.



- **[Restaurant Menu CMS prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source admin + customer-facing menu applications for managing categories, items, images, and basic analytics.



- **[QR code menu generators](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open tools for generating and hosting QR-linked digital menus without heavy commercial platforms.



- **[Static site / Markdown menu publishers](https://github.com/)**  

  Approaches that publish restaurant menus as static sites or Markdown-driven pages for full ownership and low cost.



- **[POS and menu open connectors](https://github.com/)**  

  Experimental connectors that push menu data between open POS/ERP systems and simple storefronts.



- **[Allergen and nutritional open menu extensions](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools and templates for enriching digital menus with allergen, dietary, and nutritional information.



- **[Multi-location menu open configurations](https://github.com/)**  

  Patterns for managing shared menus with location-specific pricing and availability in open systems.



- **[Online ordering open storefront starters](https://github.com/)**  

  Starter kits that combine a digital menu with basic cart and order capture for direct restaurant ordering.



- **[ERPNext / Odoo restaurant and menu modules](https://github.com/)**  

  Broader open ERP modules that include menu, recipe, and outlet catalog management for hospitality businesses.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **URY (ERPNext)** when you want menu management tightly integrated with POS, kitchen, and inventory in an open stack.

- Deploying simple **digital/QR menu web apps** for single-location restaurants that only need a publishable menu.

- Combining open menus with commercial channel managers (Deliverect, Otter, etc.) when marketplace sync is required.

- Accepting that large-scale menu synchronization across DoorDash/Uber Eats/Grubhub, advanced SEO menu pages, and turnkey marketing still favor commercial platforms (Popmenu, Owner.com, Deliverect, SinglePlatform, GloriaFood, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, avoiding per-menu or per-location fees, and custom branding.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Maintain master menu and recipes in URY/ERPNext or a simple CMS → publish a digital/QR menu → optionally push selected items to ordering channels via commercial middleware → keep photos, prices, and availability under restaurant control. Suitable for independent restaurants and small groups with technical support. Most multi-location and delivery-heavy operators continue to rely on commercial menu and channel-management platforms.
